## 1. What this covers

The **built-in graph data** of dataviz, made by cetome (the "Data"):

- `data/en18031.js`, `data/en18031-einfo.js`, `data/en303645.js`, `data/pren40000-1-3.js`,
- the same data as dataviz files: `standards/en18031.json`, `standards/en18031-einfo.json`,

The Data is cetome's work of analysis: which items EN 18031-1, -2 and -3 and their E.Info annex,
